# irenesaibene.github.io

### Welcome to my GitHub Page

I'm 21 years old and currently studying Corporate Communication at the [University of Milan](https://com.cdl.unimi.it/it).

Last year, I had the opportunity to spend my Erasmus experience in [Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/) which allowed me to discover a new culture, improve my English, and become more independent. In October, I graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Communication Sciences from the [University of Bergamo](https://www.unibg.it/studiare/corsi/offertaformativa/scienze-comunicazione).

Alongside my studies, I work as a hostess at fairs, corporate events, and private functions. One of my most memorable experiences was working with Coca-Cola during the Winter Olympics in Cortina. I've also been working at private events for Da Vittorio for the past couple of years.

These experiences have helped me develop strong communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills, as well as the ability to adapt to different environments and work with people from diverse backgrounds.
